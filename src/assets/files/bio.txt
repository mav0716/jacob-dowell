I’m Jacob Dowell, a software engineer with a foundation built on discipline, leadership, and adaptability from my four years of active duty in the United States Army. My military service gave me the unique privilege of traveling the world, representing our country as an ambassador, and learning to work seamlessly with people from diverse backgrounds. These experiences strengthened my ability to collaborate, think strategically, and stay calm under pressure — skills I carry into every professional challenge.

During my studies, I also served as a CIS department tutor, mentoring students through programming fundamentals and helping them gain confidence in their technical abilities.

Today, I’m proud to be a Software Engineer at Charles Schwab, where I design and develop scalable solutions that enhance clients’ financial management experiences. My journey from the military to technology reflects my commitment to continuous learning, innovation, and making a meaningful impact through software that empowers users.
